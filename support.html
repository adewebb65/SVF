♥ Support

Support the mission

Space Value Foundation is always open to proactive, constructive collaboration with like-minded individuals, organisations and institutions, public and private.

Our mission is to hardwire sustainability and responsibility into our financial value framework, mathematics, and monetary architecture, by introducing the dimension of space and our responsibility for impact in space into our equations of value and return, by rethinking the models that define the value of money, its logic, its creation, allocation, and deployment.

The Space Value Foundation mission

Consider joining us and supporting our work. Help us address the most fundamental barriers to humanity’s sustainable future in space.

1Why support the Foundation?

Our current financial and monetary economics, built entirely around risk and time, have led us to the edge of an ecological catastrophe. Our sustainability standards and frameworks are powerless and ineffective, as they fall short of addressing the core issue, i.e., the very logic of the value of money, and the absence of space and responsibility from our models.

By supporting the Space Value Foundation you are, in fact, advocating for an authentic debate that goes beyond the symptoms, beyond that which is convenient, and addresses the true challenge of our sustainability and expansion in space.

Our aims are twofold: education and accountability.

Aim 1

Education

If the leaders, managers, and investors of tomorrow enter the world of business with a better understanding of how the financial mathematics they apply to their projects and portfolios affects planetary sustainability, we can hard-wire better outcomes.

Aim 2

Accountability

If companies, corporations, banks, public and private institutions could report an authentic and comprehensive picture of the impact their value chain has on space and its many layers, our ability to assess and change our trajectory will be far more effective.

With education and awareness, we aim to change some of the deep-rooted dynamics that have brought us to this unhealthy situation and potentially disastrous bottleneck.

2What your support does

Not a charity

Space Value Foundation is not a charity. All monetary support we receive is treated as a membership contribution. All revenue is continuously invested and reinvested in our mission and programmes.

If you would like to support our work, either with a one-off contribution or by becoming a regular supporter, choose the route that suits you below. You can also make a direct bank transfer: bank details are available by writing to info@spacevaluefoundation.com.
